# 12B `lm_head` L1 address and performance record

Collected 2026-09-29/30 on Blackhole P150, 1x1, bf16, B=1, K=3, `fused-batch-dim` traced route. Source revision at capture was `8b0f2711696f7a69d4da88f04a7bb83714aac8b6` plus the working-tree instrumentation and K/V lifetime edit. The loaded Python extension was `build_codex_current/ttnn/_ttnn.so`; the source-tree extension link pointed at another build and was excluded using a `/tmp` package overlay. Hardware runs used `TT_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0`. Captures are diagnostic and are not used for timing.

## Allocator lifetime and capacity

The fixed reservation ends at address **111,488**, and allocatable L1 ends at **1,572,864**. Setup, prefill warmup and prefill completion show **zero** persistent L1 bytes per bank in the unpinned demo. The pinned 35,200-column head is one 655,360 B/bank WIDTH_SHARDED block at **917,504**, physically resident on all 110 cores from assistant load through the trace. The repaired 38,720-column head is 720,896 B/bank at **851,968**. No other persistent block appears in those snapshots.

The lifetime CB ceiling is **801,024** in `SdpaDecodeDeviceOperation`. A drafter SDPA event at graph index **2497** covers `(0..7, 0..3)`; the target layer 5 event at **20236** covers all **110** cores. In the repaired capture the corresponding target event is **20254**. Thus every core has **771,840 B** of head-only space above its lifetime CB ceiling. In the original 35,200-column trace, two K/V update staging tensors remained live at the target layer 5 CB event. They are each 32,768 B/bank, `HEIGHT_SHARDED`, physically on `(0..3,0)`, and allocated by `InterleavedToShardedDeviceOperation` at graph indices **19612** and **19627**. Their address intervals are **[884,736, 917,504)** and **[851,968, 884,736)**; they were deallocated at **20322** and **20325**, after the SDPA at **20236**. LOCKSTEP reserves both ranges on every bank, though only four cores contain their data. The 65,536 B/bank difference is transient K/V staging, not firmware or the 111,488-byte fixed reservation.

Before the lifetime change, the 35,200-column head leaves 771,840 - 655,360 - 65,536 = **50,944 B/bank** at the target SDPA frontier. An uninstrumented 38,720-column demo failed there: L1 allocation at **786,432** versus CB end **801,024**, a 14,592-byte overlap. `decode_forward` now deallocates the consumed K/V staging tensors immediately after both cache updates, before SDPA. The uninstrumented 38,720-column demo then passes, and its capture shows only the pinned head live at the CB ceiling. This leaves **50,944 B/bank on each of the 110 cores** between its lifetime CB frontier and the head start. The next 3,520-column uniform-shard increment costs 65,536 B/bank and cannot fit; an uninstrumented **42,240-column** demo fails at exactly **786,432** against CB end **801,024**. Current capacity is limited by that increment against the SDPA CB ceiling. The head-only space above the ceiling is **771,840 B/bank**.

HYBRID placement alone cannot enlarge a WIDTH_SHARDED head slice that must fit on every core. Before the lifetime fix, moving only LOCKSTEP accounting would not have freed the four cores that physically held K/V staging. After the fix, temporary staging has at most the 50,944-byte gap on ceiling cores, below one uniform head increment and before its own CB needs. Neighboring-L1-shard reads remain a separate sharding task.

## Performance and numerical gates

The uninstrumented 35,200-column op test matched all logits bit for bit, all 16 chained tokens, and the pinned-weight fingerprint. Full head: **1,412.73 us**; L1 slice: **41.17 us**; DRAM tail: **1,182.64 us**; two-matmul pair: **1,222.88 us**; split logits combine and argmax: **125.88 us**. A matching DRAM slice takes **190.12 us**. The DRAM tail dominates the split head; the measured L1 slice placement gain at identical config is **148.95 us**.

The 38,720-column op test also matched every logit bit for bit and all 16 chained tokens. It measured **42.64 us** for the L1 slice versus **209.10 us** for the same-config DRAM slice, **1,177.06 us** for the DRAM tail, **1,218.60 us** for the pair, and **127.63 us** for split combine plus argmax. Its separate, counterbalanced full/pair/pair/full timing measured **[1370.18, 1218.68, 1218.29, 1369.88] us**, giving paired split-minus-full deltas of **-151.50 and -151.60 us**. These are op-only pair timings; combine/argmax is measured separately and the step/E2E results below include it.

An uninstrumented tuned K=3 step ABBA run before the K/V edit measured **3.172 ms/step** with no pin and **3.001 ms/step** with 35,200 columns in both repetitions. The K/V edit affects target decode, so a new 500-token paired E2E comparison is the final end-to-end gate. The previous 35,200-column 500-token campaign used the same long prompt, route and acceptance in each arm: tuned paired deltas were **-0.54 and -0.74 ms/iteration**, and default-scope deltas were **-0.61 and -0.76 ms/iteration**. These are pre-fix source results and are not mixed into the new pairs.

The post-fix 38,720-column 500-token campaign ran eight fresh, uninstrumented processes in `0/split/split/0` order for each tuner scope. Every cell used the same source, extension, prompt and toolchain, reached `fused-traced`, generated 500 tokens, and produced the same text hash. Tuned scope had acceptance **1.08/3**, **241** iterations, and paired split-minus-base deltas **-0.77 and -0.75 ms/iteration**. Default scope had acceptance **1.09/3**, **240** iterations, and deltas **-0.70 and -0.74 ms/iteration**. The exact per-cell results and logs are in `generated/lmhead_l1_profile/e2e_500_38720_reclaimed/`.

The second matmul (DRAM tail) is already measured separately above. A one-program mixed-source head would retain its dominant tail and must preserve the current HiFi2 accumulation order and vocab-ordered logits. Its *optimistic upper bound* from removing roughly 20–25 us/step of extra split combine/argmax work is around 0.06–0.08 ms per K=3 iteration; some combine work and the dominant DRAM tail would remain. The tuned split benefit moved by up to 0.23 ms/iteration between the two separately matched 500-token campaigns, and there is no measured positive lower bound for the new program. The conditional prototype gate is therefore not established. A thin-K custom activation kernel likewise has no measured basis to beat the current ~43-us multicast slice or bit-exactness proof; it remains after the head work.

## Reproduction and artifacts

The 500-token counterbalanced runner is `run_lmhead_e2e.py --cols 38720 --output generated/lmhead_l1_profile/e2e_500_38720_reclaimed`. All timing runs exclude the graph capture knob and use fresh processes per cell.
